Specialist Modalities

Advanced Therapeutic Approaches

Beyond talk therapy — we integrate specialized, research-supported modalities to accelerate healing at deeper emotional and psychological levels.

EMDR Therapy

Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing to help partners heal from past trauma, painful memories, and emotional wounds that block closeness.

Mindfulness-Based Therapy

Grounding practices that help couples stay present, regulate emotional reactions, and respond with intention rather than impulse during conflict.

Sand Tray Therapy

A creative, non-verbal technique where clients use miniature figures and sand to externalize inner experiences that are difficult to put into words.

Narrative Therapy

Helping couples reauthor their shared story — separating problems from identity and rewriting a new, empowering relationship narrative together.

RSMarr

Evidence-based marriage and relationship therapy. Helping couples build trust, manage conflict, and restore emotional intimacy in a confidential, professional setting.
