Our 4-Step Process

What Happens During Your Therapy Journey

We follow a structured, compassionate process designed to create safety, insight, and lasting change.

STEP 1

Secure Intake & Assessment

Complete a confidential relationship assessment questionnaire. We identify your current conflict cycle, communication patterns, and core emotional needs as a foundation for therapy.

STEP 2

Cycle Mapping & De-escalation

Your therapist guides you through identifying the negative triggers and reactions that fuel conflict. Together, you learn de-escalation techniques and interrupt the cycle before it escalates.

STEP 3

Connection Building & Maintenance

Build emotional safety through guided exercises, share vulnerable feelings, and receive tools for long-term relationship resilience, intimacy, and conflict repair.
