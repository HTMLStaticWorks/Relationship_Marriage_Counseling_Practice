Who We Are

About RS Marr Counseling Practice

Learn about our story, our licensed therapists, and our unwavering commitment to restoring connection and emotional intimacy for couples.

Founded in 2011

Bridging the Gap in Emotional Intimacy for Over a Decade

RS Marr was founded with a singular mission: to create a safe, confidential space where couples and individuals can heal relational wounds and rebuild the connection they deserve. Dr. Rebecca Stern, our clinical director, saw a critical gap in accessible, evidence-based couples therapy and set out to bridge.

Confidentiality First

All sessions are 100% legally confidential — your story stays with your therapist.

Evidence-Based

We use clinically validated modalities: EFT, Gottman Method, and CBT frameworks.

Empathetic Stance

We never take sides. Each partner receives an equal, compassionate, unbiased voice.

Our Credentials

Professional Certifications & Ethical Standards

Every therapist at RS Marr meets the highest clinical and ethical standards, ensuring safe, effective, and confidential care for all clients.

AAMFT Member

American Association for Marriage & Family Therapy certified. We uphold the strictest standards of ethical family and couples therapy practice.

Gottman Certified

Our lead therapists are certified by The Gottman Institute, masters of the evidence-based Sound Relationship House framework for couples.

State Licensed

All practitioners hold active state board licenses as Licensed Marriage & Family Therapists (LMFT) or Licensed Clinical Social Workers (LCSW).

HIPAA Compliant

All records, sessions, and communications are fully HIPAA-protected. Your privacy is legally guaranteed and professionally enforced at every step.

Our Therapeutic Approach

A Safe Space for Every Couple's Unique Journey

We understand that every relationship is unique. Conflict cycles that develop over years — the pursuer-distancer dynamic, emotional shut-down, or recurring arguments — require a structured, compassionate approach to untangle. That's exactly what we provide.

Our therapists create a structured yet flexible environment, combining the research-backed Gottman Method and Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) to help you and your partner identify negative patterns, rebuild trust, and develop lasting connection habits.
